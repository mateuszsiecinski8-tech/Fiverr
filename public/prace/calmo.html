About the project

A meditation app concept designed around a single principle: zero pressure. No leaderboards, no comparing yourself to others — just you and your breath.

Screen 01Onboarding

Three simple cards instead of a form. Users start their first session in under 30 seconds.

Screen 02Home

One “session of the day” instead of paralyzing choice. Categories come below — hierarchy guides the eye.

Screen 03Profile

Stats that motivate gently: a day streak and minutes of calm instead of scoreboards.
