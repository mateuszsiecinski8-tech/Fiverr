About

I photograph slowly, and I ask a lot of questions first.

I started in newspapers, which taught me to be fast, and then spent a decade unlearning it. Most of my work now happens over days rather than hours — sitting in kitchens, waiting for the light, letting people forget the camera is there.

I shoot digital and film, almost always available light, and I edit my own work. If you need forty polished frames by Friday, I'm probably the wrong photographer. If you want six that people remember, let's talk.
